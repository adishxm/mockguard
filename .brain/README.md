## Freeze decisions

MockGuard is the final product name. The HackMatrix MVP is a focused TypeScript/Jest developer tool, not a universal test-quality platform. A dependency mock is not automatically bad. Static findings are advisory unless supported by dynamic evidence. Mutation results must show their denominator and exclusions. A surviving mutant is evidence of an unprotected change under the selected test scope, not proof that the entire test is invalid. No AI suggestion is trusted without re-running the test and mutation loop.
